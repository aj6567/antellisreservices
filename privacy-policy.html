Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 3, 2026  |  Antellis Real Estate Services LLC  |  Fort Worth, Texas
Antellis Real Estate Services LLC is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or contact us — including through SMS/text messaging.
1
Introduction

Antellis Real Estate Services LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit antellisrealestateservices.com or contact us through any channel, including SMS/text messaging.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our site and services.

2
Information We Collect
Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a property inquiry, contact form, or opt in to receive SMS communications, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number (including mobile number for SMS communications)
  • Email address
  • Property address and details
  • Message content and communication history
Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring website or source
  • Device type and operating system
3
SMS / Text Messaging Communications

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to receive text messages from Antellis Real Estate Services LLC, you consent to receive SMS communications including:

  • Property evaluation updates and offers
  • Follow-up communications regarding your property inquiry
  • Transactional messages related to our services

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and interaction level.

Rates: Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

To Opt Out: Reply STOP to any text message at any time.

For Help: Reply HELP or email info@antellisreservices.com.

No Selling: We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

No Obligation: Consent to receive SMS messages is not required as a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

4
How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your property inquiries and provide requested services
  • Send you property offers and relevant communications
  • Communicate via SMS if you have opted in
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and protect the security of our business
5
How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business, subject to confidentiality agreements
  • When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Antellis Real Estate Services LLC, our clients, or others
  • With your explicit consent
6
Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When your information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

7
Your Rights & Choices

You may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt-Out of SMS: Reply STOP to any text message at any time
  • Opt-Out of Email: Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@antellisreservices.com or call (817) 842-3400.

8
Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

9
Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

10
Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

11
Security
